## Ground rules behind the visuals

- Default to 10-series unless a point load/dynamic load proves otherwise.
- Split extrusion into: structural frame, panel/backer support, and locator/anti-rattle tie.
- Wall-side flush furniture does **not** automatically require rear 80/20; use rear rails/posts when freestanding, dynamic, drawer-loaded, or heavy service-mounted.
- Use stock lengths and connector samples for the first order; avoid exact vendor cuts until physical envelope/service gates pass.
- Do electrical as a dead mechanical mockup first: no final crimping, no energizing until access/routing is proven.
- Keep fuses, disconnects, pump/strainer, valves, and service fasteners reachable without dismantling the furniture.
