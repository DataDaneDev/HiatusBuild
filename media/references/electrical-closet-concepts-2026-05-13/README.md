# Electrical closet concept references — 2026-05-13

AI-generated visual references based on the cardboard mockup photo for the Hiatus camper electrical closet / utility core.

## Design intent captured

- Replace the cardboard mockup with a practical electrical/utility closet.
- Single approximately 24-inch-deep rectangular closet mass against the wall.
- Lower window-side corner cut away to form an under-window tabletop / charging platform.
- AC/DC/USB-C/Anderson outlets and monitor/display on the charging platform face.
- Battery, diesel heater, and diesel tank below the platform in separated service zones.
- Integrated continuation into the right-side battery-bank bench/storage.
- Butcher-block desk surface to the left under the window.

## Caveat

These are concept references only, not fabrication drawings. Validate actual dimensions, service clearances, ventilation, heater/fuel separation, AC/DC separation, cable protection, fusing, and roof-down/interior envelope before cutting material or making penetrations.
