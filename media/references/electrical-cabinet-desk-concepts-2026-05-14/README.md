# Electrical cabinet + desk concept references — 2026-05-14

AI-generated visual references for compact, realistic Hiatus camper layouts combining a driver-side front/bulkhead electrical cabinet area with a mid-driver-side computer desk/workstation.

## Design intent captured

- Keep the far driver-side/front bulkhead corner as the main electrical service zone.
- Use plywood backer boards, compact cabinets, removable panels, or service cassettes rather than polished showroom cabinetry.
- Keep batteries and dense electrical mass low where practical.
- Preserve an open center aisle and avoid adding a galley, bed platform, couch, shower, or luxury van-life furniture.
- Keep the workstation compact: fixed narrow desk, fold-down desk, simple wall-mounted work plank, or small office nook.
- Maintain realism: white camper shell, black trim/lower tub, plywood floor, roof vent, daylight, and unfinished DIY-build plausibility.

## Caveat

These are concept references only, not fabrication drawings or wiring diagrams. Validate actual dimensions, roof-down clearance, window/bed-rail clearances, service access, ventilation, battery restraint, fuse/disconnect locations, AC/DC separation, cable protection, strain relief, and fire-safe mounting before cutting material or making permanent changes.
