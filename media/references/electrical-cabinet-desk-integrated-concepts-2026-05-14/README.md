# Electrical cabinet + desk integrated concept references — 2026-05-14

AI-generated visual reference batch for additional Hiatus camper driver-side electrical-cabinet + workstation exploration. This set intentionally pushes a few more integrated furniture concepts where the electrical cabinet, low service plinth, cable chase, and mid-bed desk read as one coherent plywood module instead of separate add-ons.

## Design intent

- Driver-side front/bulkhead corner remains the primary electrical service zone.
- Batteries/dense electrical mass stay low where practical.
- Desk sits mid-bed on the same driver-side furniture spine, preserving the center aisle.
- Prefer simple plywood, black trim/angle, latches, removable panels, ventilation slots, and protected cable chases.
- Include some stronger furniture-integration variants: continuous plinths, shared rails, partition walls, removable bridge desks, and one-piece service/desk modules.
- Avoid galley, bed platform, couch, shower, luxury van-life cabinetry, and house-like finishes.
